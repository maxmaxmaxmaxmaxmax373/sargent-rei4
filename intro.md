# Rational Expectations and Inflation

*Fourth Edition*

> Thomas J. Sargent, *Rational Expectations and Inflation*, Fourth Edition.

This is a MyST / Jupyter Book edition of Thomas J. Sargent's *Rational Expectations and
Inflation*. It collects the essays of the third edition, restores the chapter on the
speculation against the Hong Kong dollar (written with David T. Beers and Neil Wallace),
and renders the text, mathematics, figures, and data tables for the web.

## About this edition

Relative to the printed third edition, this online edition:

- **Restores the Hong Kong dollar chapter** (Chapter 7), which was set aside in the third
  edition.
- **Renders all mathematics with MathJax** so equations can be searched, linked, and copied.
- **Ties data tables to their sources**: where a table is built from a spreadsheet, it is
  rendered from the underlying CSV data so it can be regenerated.
